WHAT THIS SHOWS
  1. The pipeline is closed end to end on this laptop: 10 s of video -> 7:47 of
     GVHMR -> 2.4 s of IK -> a GMR motion pickle (root_pos, root_rot wxyz, dof_pos,
     fps) = the reference-motion format imitation learning consumes.
  2. Video-derived SMPL-X is noisier than mocap, as expected: the SOURCE ankles
     drift from 0.07 m to 0.23 m over the clip while the player is on the ground
     at both ends.  That ~15 cm of GVHMR vertical drift is what the +15 / +18 cm
     float on both robots is.  It is a stage-1 property, not a retargeting one,
     and it is why a ground-height pass (or GMR's offset_human_data_to_ground)
     will be needed before these trajectories are fed to imitation learning.
  3. Servo speed is exceeded on 5 (R1) / 1 (G1) frames at 22.7 / 23.9 rad/s --
     a tennis swing is genuinely fast; same magnitude on both robots.
  4. Joint limits: 0 on both, again.  Now holds on video data too.
