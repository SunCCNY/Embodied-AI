STAGE 3b -- the ground pass: removing GVHMR's vertical drift before IK   (2026-09-17)
=====================================================================================
Problem (from week28_gvhmr_join.txt): GVHMR's world-frame output has no floor.  On
tennis.mp4 the lowest SMPL-X foot joint climbs 0.005 m (frame 0) -> 0.15 m (frame 300)
while the player is on the ground at both ends.  Shape of the drift, measured on the
source human data after GMR's y-up -> z-up rotation:
    linear fit of lowest foot joint: slope +1.62 cm/s, residual sd 1.69 cm
i.e. a clean ramp, not a step and not per-frame noise.  The robot inherited it as a
+15 cm float by the end of the clip.

Fix: `gvhmr_to_robot_headless.py --ground linear` (now the default).  On the HUMAN
frames before IK: rolling-min (+-0.5 s) lower envelope of the lowest foot joint ->
straight-line fit -> subtract per frame -> shift so the clip's lowest foot sits at
`--ground_target` (0 m).  GMR itself is untouched.  Other modes kept for comparison:
`none` (old behaviour), `const` (subtract clip minimum only), `perframe` (GMR's own
`offset_to_ground=True`, which pins the lowest foot to +0.10 m on EVERY frame).

Same checker as week 28 (physics geom selector, 1 cm contact tolerance).  All rows are
the identical 312 frames.  "drift" = slope of the robot's lowest contact point over the
clip; "1st s / last s" = its mean over the first / last 30 frames.

  r1/none       -2.67   +5.65  +15.15   41 (13.1%)  +1.28    -0.67   +11.48    22.60 cm    0   22.7    5

WHAT THIS SHOWS
  1. `linear` removes the drift: +1.28 -> +0.04 cm/s, first and last second now agree
     (+3.1 vs +3.6 cm, were -0.7 vs +11.5).  Joint limits, peak speed and the >20 rad/s
     frame count are byte-identical across all four R1 rows -- the pass only translates
     the target, it does not change the pose the IK finds.
  2. `const` is useless here: subtracting one number leaves the ramp intact (same
     +1.28 cm/s).  A constant offset is only right when the drift is zero.
  3. `perframe` (GMR's built-in) gets 0 % contact AND cuts the pelvis range from 22.6 to
     12.7 cm: it deletes the crouch at frame ~120 and the swing lift at ~240 because it
     re-zeroes every frame.  Reference motion for imitation learning must keep those.
  4. The residual +3.6 cm mean float after `linear` is GVHMR's per-frame noise (envelope
     residual sd 1.7 cm) plus genuine foot lifts, not drift.  A stance-aware pass (pin
     the foot only while it is planted) would be the next refinement; not needed to
     start IL.
  5. G1 control on the same passed frames: drift also gone (-0.01 cm/s), floats slightly
     more than the R1 (+4.54 vs +3.58 mean, 0.6 % vs 7.4 % contact) -- the tennis clip
     keeps the "R1 contacts more" direction noted on 09-16.

BUG CAUGHT DURING THE BUILD: the first version shifted the fitted line by -min instead of
+min and put the lowest foot at -8.4 cm; the `[ground] ... after` print line exposed it
immediately.  Keep that print.
