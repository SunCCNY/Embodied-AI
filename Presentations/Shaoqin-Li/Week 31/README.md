# Week 31: Walk, run and jump, the first R1 skills

- `Embodied AI Week 31.pptx`: the deck (24 slides, five embedded videos, speaker script in the slide notes). Slide 24 lists the references with links.
  Part 1 tests switching between two trained motions (tennis and dance, 2026-10-20). Part 2 is a walk/run policy trained with
  Unitree's `unitree_rl_mjlab` (2026-10-22), an action-scale bug in Unitree's shipped R1 deploy file, and delay training (2026-10-23).
  Part 3 is jumping jacks and jump rope from stock video through the Video-to-R1 pipeline (2026-10-23).
- `week31_speaker_script.md`: about 20 minutes with timings, Q&A preparation and source links.
- `week31_box_by_box.html`: speaker's companion, every text box with what it says and what it means in plain words. Open it in a browser.

Every number in the deck comes from the results READMEs, and the charts, terminal panels and videos are used unchanged:
