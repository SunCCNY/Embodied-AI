The tested ramp was inconsistent
It helped one case and hurt another. The policies never saw ramps or pauses in training, so a blended target is new to them.

What it means

Mixed results across the cells. A likely reason: a policy only knows what it saw in training, and it never saw a slow blend.

Motor springs alone did not hold the stand

Motor springs alone did not hold the stand
In one test, holding the default pose without a policy, the robot tipped over in about 3 s. The stand between skills has to be a balancing policy. That is Part 2.

What it means

Motor springs = PD control: each motor pulls toward a target angle like a spring with damping. Nothing keeps the robot balanced. This was one 3 s test.

Why it matters

Why it matters: the stand in the middle of the bag must balance by itself, so I trained one next.

What it means

This is the bridge to Part 2: the stand has to be a trained balancing policy.

What I found
